**Test Object**
`<A>` Component

**Test Goal**
Exercise the component's click callback; the assertion is inside the callback and does not independently establish that the callback runs.

**Test Activities**
1. Prepare a flag variable `io`, initialized to 0, and an `onClick` callback.
2. Render `<A data-testid="a.clickme" onClick={onClick}>click</A>`.
3. Find the element using `data-testid` and simulate a click.
4. If the callback runs, set `io` to 1 and assert that it is truthy inside the callback; no assertion after the click checks whether the callback ran.
