**Test Object**
Amazon India Product Search, Add-to-Cart, and Checkout Process

**Test Goal**
Exercise product search, product selection, adding to the cart, and clicking the checkout button; the code does not explicitly check the final checkout page or URL.

**Test Activities**
1. Open the Amazon India homepage.
2. Enter `T-shirt` in the search box and click the search button.
3. Locate the first product link in the search results, remove the `target` attribute, and click to enter the product details page.
4. Click the "Add to Cart" button to add the product to your shopping cart.
5. Click the "Proceed to checkout" button to proceed to the checkout page.
