**Test Object**
Modal `[role="dialog"]` and its launch button

**Test Goal**
Attempt to verify that the modal opens after clicking the button; the code uses a value read before the click and does not assert the result.

**Test Activities**
1. Access the homepage `/`.
2. Locate the modal and obtain its `aria-hidden` attribute.
3. Find the `"Launch demo modal"` button and click it.
4. Compare the saved, pre-click `aria-hidden` value with `"false"` and pass the result to `expect`, without calling a matcher or reading the attribute again.
