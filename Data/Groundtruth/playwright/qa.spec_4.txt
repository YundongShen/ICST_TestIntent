**Test Object**
Coverage/chart elements on the QA page

**Test Goal**
Check that the count of elements related to coverage or charts is greater than or equal to zero; this permits an empty result and does not establish that these elements are present.

**Test Activities**
1. Locate all SVG, canvas, or class elements containing `chart`, `coverage`, or `progress`.
2. Get the number of elements.
3. Assert that the number is greater than or equal to 0.
