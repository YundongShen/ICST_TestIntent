**Test Object**
Homepage RSS Link

**Test Goal**
Locate the homepage RSS link and check that its Locator object is truthy; this does not verify that the link exists in the DOM.

**Test Activities**
1. Open the homepage `/`.
2. Create a Locator using the role "link" and name "RSS".
3. Assert that the Locator object is truthy, without checking whether it matches a DOM element.
