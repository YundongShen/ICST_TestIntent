**Test Object**
`POPULATE.POPULATE` function

**Test Goal**
Verify that, under the Poisson distribution, the output x matches the input, the shape of y is correct, and the mean is close to the `poisson_events` parameter.

**Test Activities**
1. Construct a vector `x` from 0 to 99999.
2. Call `POPULATE.POPULATE` with parameters `distribution="poisson"` and `poisson_events=1`.
3. Check that `res.x` and `x.v` are completely identical, and `res.y.shape` is correct.
4. Verify that the absolute value of the difference between the mean of `res.y` and 1 is less than 0.015.
