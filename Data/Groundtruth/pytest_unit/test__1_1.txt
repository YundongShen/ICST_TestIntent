**Test Object**
The `myCode.build_url` function

**Test Goal**
Verify that, under a mock `BASE_URL`, `build_url` can construct the correct query string based on the passed price range and sorting parameters.

**Test Activities**
1. Temporarily replace `myCode.BASE_URL` with `'http://localhost/products'` using a mocker.
2. Call `myCode.build_url`.
3. Assert that the returned URL equals `'http://localhost/products?minPrice=100&maxPrice=500&order=desc'`.
