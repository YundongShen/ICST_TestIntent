**Test Object**
The `ADD.ADD` function

**Test Goal**
Verify that when given two vectors, the function correctly calculates the sum of their corresponding elements.

**Test Activities**
1. Construct a vector `x` with values ​​from -10 to 9.
2. Construct a vector `y` with values ​​from -20 to 18, with a step size of 2.
3. Call `ADD.ADD(a=x, b=[y])`.
4. Use NumPy to assert that the returned vector `res.v` is equal to `x.v + y.v`.
