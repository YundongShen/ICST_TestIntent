**Test Object**
The `cli.parseCozyURL` function

**Test Goal**
Verify that the function correctly removes the trailing forward slash when the URL has one at the end.

**Test Activities**
1. Prepare a URL with a trailing forward slash: `'https://toto.mycozy.cloud/'`.
2. Call `cli.parseCozyURL(urlIn)`.
3. Assert the return value to `'https://toto.mycozy.cloud'` without the forward slashes.
